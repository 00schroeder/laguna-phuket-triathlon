Summary

The race weekend brought about 1,650 people and an estimated 37 million baht (US$1.1 million) into the Laguna and Bang Tao area in 2025.

This is an outside view. It is built from the official results, published prices and stated assumptions, with no internal data from the organiser.

  • About 1,060 people finished a race over the 2024 weekend, the latest year with a results list for every race.
  • Four in five triathlon finishers lived outside Thailand in 2025, against one in four in 2022.
  • Rooms are the largest cost, at about 19 of the 37 million baht in 2025.
  • The 2025 range is 14 to 102 million baht, because nights and companions are assumed.
  • Registration and hotel figures from the organiser would turn the estimate into a measurement.

People at the race weekend

About 1,060 people finished a race over the 2024 weekend, in line with the organiser's "more than 1,000".

  • Triathlon, sprint and duathlon
  • Relay members, three per team
  • Runs
  • Open water swims

Run results were published for 2017 to 2019 and 2024 only, so the other years show less than the whole weekend.

Triathlon field by year

The triathlon, sprint and duathlon had 557 finishers in 2025, against 700 in 2019.

  • Triathlon
  • Sprint
  • Duathlon

The main race fell from 537 finishers in 2019 to 347 in 2025. The sprint stayed between 163 and 211 outside 2020.

Where athletes live

In 2025, 81% of finishers lived outside Thailand (449 of 557), against 26% in 2022.

  • Living in Thailand
  • Other countries
  • No country recorded

Finishers by country of residence, 2025

Visitors from abroad stay longer and spend more, so estimated spending rose while the field got smaller. Results before 2024 may have been recorded differently.

Spending brought to the area

Athletes and the people with them spent an estimated 37 million baht (US$1.13 million) in the area in 2025.

Rooms are just over half of it. Most athletes stay in the Laguna hotels or in rented condos and guesthouses nearby.

Spending by year

The middle estimate runs from 32 to 40 million baht a year for 2022 to 2025.

Million baht: low, middle and high estimate

The low and high ends put every low or every high assumption together, so they are outer limits.
